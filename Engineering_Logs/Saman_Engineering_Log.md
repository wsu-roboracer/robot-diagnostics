# Engineering Log - Dashboard Mockup


## Work Completed

- Created a mockup for the RoboRacer diagnostic dashboard using **Adobe Express**.
- Focused on making subsystem status easy to understand at a glance.
- Designed sections to show the health and status of different robot components.
- Included status categories such as:
  - **Healthy**
  - **Degraded**
  - **Failed**
  - **Unknown**
- Focused the layout on helping users quickly identify which subsystem may have a problem.

## Feedback

- Shared the mockup with members of the research team.
- Received informal feedback about the layout and information shown.
- Have **not yet tested** the dashboard with someone outside of the team.

## Next Steps

- Test the mockup with someone who is not familiar with the project.
- Observe whether they can:
  - Understand the subsystem statuses.
  - Identify which component has a problem.
  - Understand the difference between **Failed** and **Unknown**.
  - Understand the dashboard without much explanation.
- Record any confusing parts or missing information.
- Update the mockup based on the feedback.
